## Hero recommendation

Use `hero-bmw-wide.webp` (1600×1000, about 242 KB) as a close crop of the owner's BMW paint finish. It emphasizes crisp navy reflections and a headlamp, works with the logo's blue palette, and has no prominent license plate. A dark gradient behind left-aligned copy can preserve legibility. `hero-bmw.webp` retains the full 3:4 photograph (1500×2000, about 506 KB); the original camera was intentionally tilted. On mobile, a portrait crop is preferable to forcing the wide image.

A second option, `hero-altima-wide.webp` (1600×1000, about 297 KB), shows the complete gray Nissan Altima against palms. It is authentic but has a busier residential background and is less distinctive. `hero-altima.webp` is the corresponding portrait version. The initial working filename hero-lexus was corrected after full-resolution visual identification; no such obsolete file remains.

No supplied video is suitable as a full-width desktop hero without aggressive cropping. A still hero plus lightweight, click-to-play portfolio clips is a better experience with the current source material.

## Delivery and verification

HEIC decoding was done using the bundled software decoder. macOS `sips` produced black output for these sources in this environment, which was discovered in contact-sheet inspection; none of those failed exports were delivered. All delivered WebP images were generated from the successfully decoded real photos, with EXIF orientation normalized, metadata stripped, downsampling only, and no AI editing or replacement. Videos were decoded using a temporary software decoder, exported to universally supported H.264/yuv420p MP4 (the interior clip was subsequently reduced to 1,393,973 bytes with CRF 29 while retaining 720×1280 resolution), stripped of audio and source metadata, and marked for progressive playback with faststart. Native macOS hardware decoding was unavailable in the sandbox; software decoding succeeded.

Original full-resolution photos and MOV files remain temporary audit materials in `/tmp/madan-source-media` and are not committed to the public website. The original-site AI-looking images likewise remain outside the delivered bundle. Portfolio captions should describe visible work without inventing package names, client testimonials, location, date, paint correction, coating applications, or quantified results. Images do not independently establish those details.

## Final publication selection

The release was narrowed to 30 website assets, including ten gallery photographs and their responsive thumbnails, the BMW hero/social crop, a Chevrolet service-page photograph, two video clips/posters, two fonts and the supplied logo. Unselected optimized media was retained outside the public repository in temporary audit storage. The rear-plate Infiniti, dark Audi, owner-reflection image and marine image are not published. The media manifest remains the complete audit inventory, not a claim that every audited file appears on the website.
