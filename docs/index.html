All your questions↗
Do I need to drop off my car?

No. We are a mobile detailing service. Share your home or workplace location when requesting an estimate, and we’ll confirm access and the details for your appointment.

Where do you offer mobile detailing?

We’re based in Miami Lakes and serve Miami-Dade and Broward, including Miami, Hialeah, Doral, Coral Gables, Pembroke Pines and Fort Lauderdale. Send your address or ZIP code so we can confirm availability.

How much does a detail cost?

Your estimate is based on the vehicle, its condition and the work you need. Tell us what you drive, share a few photos and explain your priorities. We’ll discuss the scope before you book.

Can you help me choose a service?

Absolutely. Start with what matters most: the cabin, the exterior, leather care or a more complete refresh. If you’re unsure, tell us what you want to improve and we’ll help you choose.
