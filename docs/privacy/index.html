This page explains what happens when you browse this site or get in touch with Madan Wash & Detail.

Last updated: October 16, 2026.

Browsing this website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not operate an account system, use advertising trackers, or collect information through a website database. GitHub may process technical information needed to host the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Requesting an estimate

The estimate and client-login links open Jobber, a separate service. Information you submit there is handled through Jobber and Madan’s business workflow. Review Jobber’s privacy policy before providing information.

Preparing a text

The text preparation tool creates a message on your device. This website does not send or store the form’s contents. Selecting “Open messaging app” passes the prepared message to your device’s messaging application. Review it and choose whether to send it. Your messaging provider’s normal policies and charges apply.

Calling, texting or emailing

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to share through that channel. Include only details needed to discuss your vehicle and appointment. Questions about your enquiry or information can be sent to info@madanwashanddetail.com.

Photos and videos

Portfolio photos and videos are served as local website files. Videos play only when you choose to play them. No social-media embeds or third-party video player are required to browse the portfolio.
