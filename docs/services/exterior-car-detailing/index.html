Care for the finish you see every day.

A clean exterior changes the way a car feels before you even open the door. Our mobile exterior detailing focuses on the dirt, road grime and environmental buildup that dull the finish. Washing, clay bar treatment and steam cleaning give us different ways to address that buildup, with optional wax or sealant protection to complete the care. Tell us about your vehicle and any areas of concern. We will discuss the work that makes sense for the finish in front of us.

A good starting point for

Vehicles that need an exterior refresh or more attention to accumulated grime. It is also a good place to start if you want to discuss ongoing appearance care without booking a full interior and exterior service.

The focus of your service
  • Exterior washing and cleaning
  • Clay bar and steam treatments discussed by condition
  • Optional wax or sealant protection

Share photos of scratches, etching or worn paint when you enquire. Those concerns deserve a separate assessment so the expected finish and any optional protection are clear before your appointment.

What can exterior detailing address?

Exterior care focuses on dirt, grime and environmental buildup that hide the finish. Scratches, etching and damaged paint need their own assessment; share your concerns so we can discuss a realistic result.

Can I add paint protection?

Yes. Optional wax or sealant protection can be discussed with your exterior detail. We will help you consider the appropriate option and include your choice in the estimate.

How long should I allow?

Allow approximately 1–3 hours depending on the vehicle and its condition. Your estimate is the place to confirm the scope and expected time for your appointment.
