A cabin you’ll want to settle into.

The cabin is where you experience your car most. Seats, carpets and the surfaces you touch every day deserve more than a quick wipe. Madan brings interior detailing to your home or workplace in Miami Lakes and our South Florida service areas. From everyday buildup to a cabin that needs deeper attention, the right starting point is your vehicle’s condition. Tell us what you want to address so we can recommend the scope before you commit.

A good starting point for

Everyday cars, family SUVs and trucks that need attention inside. Choose this service when the cabin is the priority, whether you are catching up after a busy stretch or asking about ongoing interior maintenance.

The focus of your service
  • Deep interior cleaning and shampooing
  • Attention to fabric, leather and interior surfaces

How long does an interior detail take?

A typical interior detail takes 1–3 hours. Vehicle size and condition change the scope, so ask us to confirm the expected time with your estimate.

Can I arrange recurring interior care?

Yes. Keep the cabin feeling cared for with recurring interior maintenance. Tell us how you use your car and the frequency you have in mind so we can discuss a practical care routine.

What if there is a severe odor or contamination?

We can discuss heavy contamination and odor concerns. Describe the issue and share relevant photos when you enquire so we can assess the condition and recommend the next step.
