What to know before your detail. If your car needs something specific, give us a call and we’ll talk it through.

Your mobile appointment

Do I need to drop off my car?

No. We are a mobile detailing service. Share your home or workplace location when requesting an estimate, and we’ll confirm access and the details for your appointment.

Where do you offer mobile detailing?

We’re based in Miami Lakes and serve Miami-Dade and Broward, including Miami, Hialeah, Doral, Coral Gables, Pembroke Pines and Fort Lauderdale. Send your address or ZIP code so we can confirm availability.

How much does a detail cost?

Your estimate is based on the vehicle, its condition and the work you need. Tell us what you drive, share a few photos and explain your priorities. We’ll discuss the scope before you book.

Can you help me choose a service?

Absolutely. Start with what matters most: the cabin, the exterior, leather care or a more complete refresh. If you’re unsure, tell us what you want to improve and we’ll help you choose.

What should I prepare before my appointment?

Remove personal belongings and let us know about access rules at your home, community or workplace. Confirm parking and any requirements with us before the visit. Mention significant stains, odors or contamination in advance.

When can I book?

Business hours are Tuesday–Saturday, 10 AM–5 PM. We are closed Sunday and Monday. Request your preferred date through Jobber, or call us to discuss availability. An appointment is confirmed after we review your request.

Interior car detailing ↗

How long does an interior detail take?

A typical interior detail takes 1–3 hours. Vehicle size and condition change the scope, so ask us to confirm the expected time with your estimate.

Can I arrange recurring interior care?

Yes. Keep the cabin feeling cared for with recurring interior maintenance. Tell us how you use your car and the frequency you have in mind so we can discuss a practical care routine.

What if there is a severe odor or contamination?

We can discuss heavy contamination and odor concerns. Describe the issue and share relevant photos when you enquire so we can assess the condition and recommend the next step.

Exterior car detailing ↗

What can exterior detailing address?

Exterior care focuses on dirt, grime and environmental buildup that hide the finish. Scratches, etching and damaged paint need their own assessment; share your concerns so we can discuss a realistic result.

Can I add paint protection?

Yes. Optional wax or sealant protection can be discussed with your exterior detail. We will help you consider the appropriate option and include your choice in the estimate.

How long should I allow?

Allow approximately 1–3 hours depending on the vehicle and its condition. Your estimate is the place to confirm the scope and expected time for your appointment.

Leather care & conditioning ↗

What if the leather is cracked or worn?

Cleaning and conditioning care for the surface. Tears, cracks and worn coatings need their own assessment and may call for repair. A few clear photos will help us understand what you are looking to address.

Can you treat every type of seat material?

Tell us your vehicle model and share a photo if you are unsure whether it has leather, coated leather or a synthetic surface. The appropriate method depends on the actual material and its condition.

How often should I book leather care?

The right interval depends on your vehicle, use and condition. Ask about a practical care schedule during your estimate rather than applying the same interval to every cabin.

Engine bay & heavy contamination cleaning ↗

What should I mention about the engine bay?

Tell us about known faults, leaks, warning lights, damaged components or modifications. Cleaning addresses buildup; a mechanical concern should be assessed by an appropriate repair professional before cleaning is arranged.

How do I enquire about heavy contamination?

Contact us first and describe the issue, where it is concentrated and any known cause. We will discuss the condition with you before agreeing on a suitable scope for an appointment.

What information helps with an estimate?

Include the vehicle make and model, your city, photos where appropriate, and a clear description of the buildup, odor or contamination. Mention known engine-bay issues if you are requesting that service.

Vehicle rejuvenation ↗

How is this different from an interior detail?

An interior detail focuses on the cabin. Rejuvenation considers the interior and exterior together, so you can discuss the overall vehicle and agree on the treatments that belong in one scope.

How is the rejuvenation service planned?

Your vehicle’s condition and priorities guide the estimate. We will discuss the interior and exterior work together, including any optional protection, so the scope and expected appointment time are clear.

Do you come to my location?

Yes. We are a mobile detailing service based in Miami Lakes, serving Miami-Dade and Broward. Share your city and proposed service location when requesting your estimate so we can discuss the visit.
